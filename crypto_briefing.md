# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-02 16:01

The digital asset landscape is undergoing a structural transformation as the Digital Asset Market CLARITY Act advances to formal Senate deliberations after securing a bipartisan 15-9 vote in the Senate Banking Committee. This legislation is cementing clear regulatory boundaries, defining split jurisdictions between the SEC and CFTC, which in turn reduces execution risks for developers building native smart contracts on Bitcoin. Simultaneously, the institutional adoption of Bitcoin has matured past the speculative retail phase, with over $100B in ETF AUM settling into slow, methodical corporate treasury allocations.
