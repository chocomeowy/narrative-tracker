# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-25 16:01

The cryptocurrency market is experiencing a pronounced phase of institutional integration, primarily driven by Bitcoin's established role as a digital asset class, evidenced by sustained ETF inflows and increasing corporate treasury adoption. Ethereum continues its dominance as the leading smart contract platform, though the emergence of decidable smart contract languages like Clarity for Bitcoin is creating a new competitive dynamic. This shift signifies a move towards more predictable and secure on-chain programmability, directly challenging the established EVM architecture and indicating a significant structural evolution in how smart contracts are developed and deployed.
