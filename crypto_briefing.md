# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-11 16:01

In September 2026, the digital asset ecosystem is experiencing a critical structural realignment driven by the upcoming Senate procedural votes on the Digital Asset Market CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633). This legislation is providing the essential legal blueprints to formalize smart contract deployments, catalyzing a direct architectural challenge to Ethereum's EVM dominance through native, non-custodial Bitcoin programmability. While institutional interest in Spot Bitcoin ETFs has entered a clear Fatigue stage characterized by the first calendar year of net outflows and a plateau around $100B AUM, capital allocation is shifting toward infrastructure. Trust-minimized cross-chain liquidity networks, such as THORChain and Zeus Network, are rapidly expanding to bridge the gap between BTC and ETH without traditional custodial wrapping risks, establishing a highly integrated multi-chain environment.
