# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-02 04:01

The digital asset sector is undergoing crucial structural shifts across regulation, layer-1 infrastructure, and cross-chain execution. In the U.S. Senate, the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act faces critical friction as ethics provisions and conflict-of-interest debates stall legislative momentum, putting the 2026 window at risk and creating compliance uncertainty. On the technological front, Ethereum reinforces its EVM settlement dominance following the Fusaka upgrade, expanding data capacity while maintaining burn mechanisms. Simultaneously, native Bitcoin programmability powered by deterministic languages like Clarity is altering the competitive landscape against EVM chains. By coupling deterministic smart contracts with non-custodial liquidity protocols like THORChain, Symbiosis, and deBridge, native BTC can flow into decentralized finance applications without relying on centralized wrapped bridges.
