# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-29 10:01

As of late August 2026, the U.S. legislative landscape is the primary driver of institutional strategy. The Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (H.R. 3633) has transitioned from a theoretical framework to a central pillar of regulatory debate, forcing institutions to move past the initial 'ETF adoption' phase. We are observing a structural rotation where capital is exiting passive, stagnant spot Bitcoin ETFs in favor of active, high-utility smart contract ecosystems. This creates a direct competitive dynamic: Ethereum remains the incumbent for dApp development, while Bitcoin is increasingly evaluated as a 'secure-settlement' layer for deterministic smart contracts, enabled by new cross-chain, non-custodial liquidity protocols that effectively eliminate traditional, high-risk wrapped-token bridge models.
