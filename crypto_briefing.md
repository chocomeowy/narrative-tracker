# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-01 10:01

Despite updated legislative drafts for the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act introduced in the Senate Banking Committee, federal regulation remains stalled in procedural revisions, leaving administrative agencies like the SEC and CFTC to drive policy via enforcement. Institutional Bitcoin adoption has entrenched into a mature consolidation phase, with spot ETFs and corporate treasuries maintaining over $100 billion in AUM, reducing speculative volatility in favor of balance-sheet integration. Concurrently, technological momentum has pivoted toward non-EVM smart contract paradigms, where decidable, non-Turing complete languages such as Clarity offer deterministic execution and reentrancy resistance for Bitcoin layers. Furthermore, trust-minimized cross-chain liquidity networks like THORChain and Zeus Network are actively bridging Bitcoin and Ethereum native assets without reliance on centralized or custodial wrapped tokens.
