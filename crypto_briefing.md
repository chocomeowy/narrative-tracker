# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-05 16:00

The market structure of digital assets is undergoing a profound regulatory and architectural transformation. Legislative momentum surrounding the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (H.R. 3633) is fast approaching critical Senate consideration, providing a statutory foundation that delineates SEC and CFTC jurisdictions. This legal certainty is reshaping base-layer competition, enabling native smart contract environments on Bitcoin to directly challenge EVM supremacy without relying on synthetic or wrapped asset wrappers. Simultaneously, institutional Bitcoin adoption has transitioned into a mature, low-volatility phase dominated by $100B+ in spot ETF AUM, structured prime services, and systematic treasury allocations. In parallel, cross-chain DeFi is migrating away from vulnerable centralized bridges toward trust-minimized, non-custodial liquidity protocols facilitating direct native BTC-to-ETH asset transfers.
