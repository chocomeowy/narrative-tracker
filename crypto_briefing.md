# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-12 10:01

This landmark bipartisan bill establishes crucial jurisdictional boundaries between the SEC and CFTC while securing legal safe harbors for non-custodial smart contract protocols. This statutory shift is directly fostering a new era of native Bitcoin programmability, challenging the historical dominance of Ethereum's EVM. This shift reflects an institutional pivot toward trust-minimized, decentralized cross-chain liquidity networks like THORChain and Symbiosis, which route native BTC directly into EVM assets without relying on custodial wrapped bridges.
