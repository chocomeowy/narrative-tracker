# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-08 04:01

The digital asset sector is undergoing a structural transformation as the U.S. Senate prepares for its September 15, 2026 vote on the CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633). This impending regulatory certainty is accelerating native Bitcoin programmability and non-EVM smart contract deployment, directly challenging Ethereum's long-standing smart contract dominance. Simultaneously, institutional Bitcoin integration has entered a mature consolidation phase, marked by cooling momentum and the first calendar year of spot ETF net outflows in early 2026. In response to fragmented ecosystem liquidity, cross-chain DeFi is actively shifting away from vulnerable legacy custodial bridges toward trust-minimized, non-custodial cross-chain liquidity networks that facilitate seamless BTC-to-ETH interoperability.
