# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-23 16:01

The crypto intelligence landscape is currently shaped by a divergence between US regulatory stagnation, particularly concerning the CLARITY Act, and significant advancements in cross-chain interoperability. While legislative efforts in the US have hit procedural roadblocks, leading to "Fatigue" in regulatory development, innovation is surging in Layer 1 and interoperability sectors. Ethereum continues to solidify its position as the dominant smart contract platform, maintaining "Peak Hype" due to its established ecosystem and Proof-of-Stake transition. Concurrently, "Breakthrough" innovation is occurring in trust-minimized, native cross-chain liquidity solutions, enabling direct Bitcoin swaps into the Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) ecosystem, bypassing the need for wrapped assets. This highlights a shift towards more direct and secure interoperability, driven by developer and capital focus away from the stalled regulatory front.
