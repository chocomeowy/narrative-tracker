The digital asset landscape in late August 2026 is anchored by the legislative progress of the CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633), which has successfully merged Senate Banking and Agriculture Committee proposals. This regulatory baseline is unlocking institutional demand, transitioning spot Bitcoin ETFs and corporate treasury allocations from speculative assets to normalized macro-portfolio fixtures.
