# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-04 10:01

As of late 2026, the crypto asset market is shaped by the maturation of institutional Bitcoin adoption via regulated ETFs and treasury tools, paired with Ethereum's ongoing dominance as a decentralized settlement layer. Cross-chain interoperability between Bitcoin and Ethereum continues to advance, bringing trust-minimized bridges and native swaps to unify capital and programmable utility.
