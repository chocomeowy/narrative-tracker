# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-25 04:01

The crypto asset landscape is defined by institutional capital integration via spot Bitcoin ETFs and structural advancements in cross-chain interoperability between Bitcoin and Ethereum. While Ethereum maintains its foundational dominance in smart contract execution, non-custodial cross-chain protocols are directly connecting native Bitcoin capital into Ethereum DeFi ecosystems without wrapped token dependencies.
