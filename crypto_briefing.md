# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-06 04:01

The digital asset landscape is undergoing a structural realignment as the U.S. Senate prepares to initiate the formal legislative process for the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (H.R. 3633) on September 15, 2026. This landmark regulatory progress accelerates the transition of native Bitcoin programmability into a viable competitor to EVM dominance. By establishing clear regulatory guardrails, the Act empowers developers to build native execution layers directly on Bitcoin without relying on high-risk synthetic assets or centralized bridge structures. Simultaneously, institutional Bitcoin adoption has consolidated into a mature, low-volatility phase characterized by over $100 billion in ETF AUM and systemic corporate treasury integrations. In response to traditional bridge vulnerabilities, cross-chain DeFi is transitioning toward trust-minimized, non-custodial liquidity networks that enable native multi-chain swaps, reshaping the base-layer competition between Bitcoin and Ethereum.
