# Crypto Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-20 10:01

In late 2026, the digital asset ecosystem is experiencing a combination of structural base-layer consolidation and heightened regulatory friction. Ethereum maintains its position as the foundational settlement layer for decentralized finance following its proof-of-stake transition, while cross-chain interoperability protocols continue to dismantle reliance on wrapped assets. Meanwhile, the legislative landscape surrounding smart contract frameworks has hit a temporary roadblock, notably with the Senate's failure to advance the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act, delaying overarching regulatory clarity for digital asset market structures.
