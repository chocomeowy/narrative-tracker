# Narrative Intelligence Briefing - 2026-06-23 05:50

The AI landscape is currently defined by a transition from experimental agentic frameworks to standardized enterprise deployments, with Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Salesforce positioning autonomous agents as the new operating paradigm for business processes.
