# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-28 16:01

International corporate tax governance in late 2026 is driven by the steady operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax rules, resulting in widespread harmonization of corporate tax rates and the restructuring of domestic incentives. Simultaneously, tax authorities worldwide are enforcing mandatory real-time electronic invoicing and continuous transaction controls (CTCs) to close indirect tax gaps, while aggressive geopolitical tariff re-alignments and cross-border trade adjustments continue to challenge multinational supply chain strategies.
