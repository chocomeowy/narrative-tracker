# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-10 22:01

The late 2026 tax and trade landscape is defined by the operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum corporate tax, with over 60 countries actively implementing these rules for multinational enterprises (MNEs) exceeding the  750 million revenue threshold.
