# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-15 04:01

As of late 2026, global tax policy is firmly anchored in structural enforcement, real-time digital compliance, and the institutionalization of the OECD Pillar Two global minimum tax. Statutory corporate tax rates, led by the US federal baseline of 21%, remain remarkably stable across major jurisdictions, ending decades of downward competition. Meanwhile, multinational enterprises are heavily re-engineering their compliance systems to manage the 15% GloBE rules and adapting to regional shifts in ASEAN, where traditional tax holidays are being phased out in favor of reinvestment allowances. Concurrently, tax authorities worldwide are accelerating mandatory digital tax administration and real-time e-invoicing mandates, such as Singapore's InvoiceNow/Peppol frameworks, to eliminate VAT/GST leakages and automate corporate auditing.
