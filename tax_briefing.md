# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-22 22:01

Global tax policy is currently defined by the maturity of the OECD Pillar Two framework and an intensified focus on digital tax administration. The operationalization of the 15% minimum tax is now systemic, while regional tax authorities in ASEAN and beyond are aggressively digitizing compliance via mandatory real-time e-invoicing networks like Singapore's InvoiceNow. Simultaneously, the global trade landscape is experiencing significant volatility, with a marked resurgence in protectionist tariff policies, compelling multinational corporations to adopt more sophisticated and agile tax and supply chain management strategies.
