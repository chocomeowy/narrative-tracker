# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-04-26 16:02

## Intelligence Map Update - April 26, 2026

**Executive Briefing:**

The global tax landscape remains highly dynamic, dominated by the ongoing implementation of Pillar Two and escalating trade tensions. Pillar Two, now enforced across over 140 jurisdictions, is fundamentally reshaping corporate tax planning for multinational enterprises (MNEs) with revenues exceeding €750 million. The focus is shifting from avoidance to compliance and accurate data collection for top-up tax calculations. Simultaneously, the resurgence of trade tariffs, particularly from the US, introduces significant uncertainty, compounded by the pending Supreme Court ruling on their legality. Singapore continues to position itself as a competitive hub, leveraging its 17% corporate tax rate and attractive incentives, while simultaneously embracing digital tax transformation through its InvoiceNow e-invoicing mandate. The expansion of e-invoicing initiatives globally, and specifically in Singapore, is a key trend, driven by both compliance requirements and efficiency gains. Businesses must prioritize monitoring these developments, particularly the Supreme Court’s tariff decision and the complexities of Pillar Two implementation, to proactively mitigate risks and capitalize on emerging opportunities. The increasing focus on CSR and ESG reporting is also becoming a significant factor in corporate tax strategy.
