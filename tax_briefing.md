# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-26 16:01

The late 2026 tax landscape is increasingly defined by the intersection of stabilized global corporate income tax rates, enforced Pillar Two compliance, and a resurgence of protectionist trade policies. While multinational enterprises navigate the administrative burden of the 15% global minimum tax, governments are shifting policy leverage toward substance-based R&D incentives and the adoption of mandatory real-time electronic invoicing to close revenue gaps. Simultaneously, the introduction of new import tariff regimes is creating significant cross-border volatility, forcing corporations to rethink supply chain tax efficiency and regional fiscal strategies, particularly in the ASEAN market.
