# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-15 10:01

As of late 2026, the global corporate tax landscape has crystallized into a stable regime defined by the 15% Pillar Two minimum tax, which is now moving from legislative adoption to active enforcement. Statutory corporate tax rates have plateaued, with significant downward competition largely halted by international consensus. However, a new volatility factor has emerged: the rapid deployment of import tariffs by major economies, creating a bifurcated policy environment where corporate tax administration is becoming increasingly digitized and standardized, even as trade policy trends toward protectionism and friction.
