# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-07 10:01

The corporate tax landscape in 2026 is defined by the operationalization of the OECD's Pillar Two global minimum tax, with over 60 countries actively implementing GloBE rules and tracking compliance. Simultaneously, digital tax administration continues its rapid evolution through real-time transaction tracking and mandatory electronic invoicing frameworks, such as Singapore's InvoiceNow initiative. Across regional economic hubs like ASEAN, tax authorities are aggressively realigning withholding taxes and targeted financial incentives to preserve foreign investment competitiveness under the new 15% minimum tax framework.
