# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-13 16:01

The late 2026 corporate and international tax landscape is characterized by the operational enforcement of the OECD Pillar Two 15% minimum tax framework across 140 jurisdictions, the acceleration of real-time digital tax enforcement via structured e-invoicing mandates like Singapore's InvoiceNow, and mounting legal and structural instability in global trade tariffs. Multinationals face growing continuous compliance demands alongside domestic tax rebate interventions designed to preserve competitiveness.
