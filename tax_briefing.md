# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-26 10:01

The late 2026 corporate tax environment is defined by the full operationalization of OECD Pillar Two global minimum tax rules across over 60 jurisdictions, establishing a 15% effective tax floor for multinational enterprises. As nominal statutory corporate tax rate cuts stabilize worldwide, governments are pivoting tax competition toward substance-based R&D incentives and localized regional relief frameworks, particularly across ASEAN economies. Simultaneously, tax authorities are expanding mandatory real-time electronic invoicing, Continuous Transaction Controls (CTCs), and digital GST reporting to minimize revenue leakage and modernize compliance auditing.
