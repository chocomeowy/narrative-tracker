# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-09 04:01

The 2026 corporate tax landscape is defined by the operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax, backed by rigorous tracking of multinational effective tax rates. In tandem, countries are broadly adapting their tax structures, with significant statutory and withholding tax adjustments taking effect globally, particularly across the ASEAN region. Furthermore, real-time transactional reporting is accelerating through the standardization of e-invoicing frameworks and digital invoice management platforms to automate corporate compliance and protect domestic tax bases.
