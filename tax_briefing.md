# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-07-30 22:01

Global corporate tax policy in mid-2026 is defined by the full statutory operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax across major international jurisdictions, creating a mandatory global tax floor for multinational corporations. To protect domestic investment while remaining compliant, regional hubs like Singapore and ASEAN partners are deploying targeted tax structure adjustments, such as CIT rebates and structured cash grants. Concurrently, global commerce faces severe trade tariff volatility driven by expanded U.S. Section 232 duties on metals, new pharmaceutical tariffs, and unilateral retaliatory measures. To improve enforcement and streamline revenue collection, tax authorities are accelerating digital tax compliance through mandated Peppol-based e-invoicing networks like Singapore's InvoiceNow initiative.
