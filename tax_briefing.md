# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-10 16:01

Global corporate tax policy as of late 2026 remains dominated by the stabilization of statutory income tax rates and the aggressive operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two framework. Multinational enterprises are navigating a dual pressure environment: the shift toward real-time digital tax administration (e-invoicing) and the increasing complexity of international trade tariffs. Policy focus has pivoted from rate-based competition toward base protection through advanced compliance automation and targeted regional fiscal incentives in hubs like ASEAN.
