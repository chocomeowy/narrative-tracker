# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-23 16:01

The global corporate tax environment in late 2026 is defined by a dichotomy of regulatory harmonization and protectionist trade policy. While the OECD's Pillar Two global minimum tax continues to operationalize, significantly impacting multinational tax planning, recent U.S.-led tariff actions have introduced notable volatility into global trade. Simultaneously, digital tax compliance—led by aggressive real-time e-invoicing mandates—is becoming the operational standard for tax authorities in Southeast Asia and beyond, effectively shifting focus from rate competition to digital audit transparency.
