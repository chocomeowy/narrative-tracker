# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-28 04:01

In late 2026, international corporate tax governance is defined by the operationalization of OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax rules alongside widespread realignment of statutory corporate tax rates and incentives, particularly across ASEAN and major global markets. Tax administrations are rapidly mandating system-to-system continuous transaction controls and digital e-invoicing framework initiatives (such as Singapore's GST InvoiceNow and Peppol networks) to secure indirect tax compliance. Concurrently, strategic geopolitical trade tariffs and targeted cross-border supply chain duty recalibrations continue to force multinational enterprises to re-evaluate international trade architecture.
