# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-02 10:01

Global corporate tax and trade policy in 2026 is driven by the operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15 percent global minimum tax across major jurisdictions, amidst localized political frictions and implementation complexities. Concurrently, sovereign nations maintain economic competitiveness through tailored corporate tax incentives and territorial exemptions, exemplified by Singapore's 17 percent headline tax regime coupled with strategic tax rebates.
