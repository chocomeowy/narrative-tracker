# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-10 22:01

Global corporate tax policy in late 2026 is characterized by the convergence of OECD Pillar Two minimum tax implementation, statutory rate stabilization, and aggressive transition toward real-time digital tax administration. Tax authorities across key economic corridors, particularly within ASEAN, are replacing traditional headline rate competition with targeted structural incentives and mandatory real-time electronic invoicing—such as Singapore's GST InvoiceNow Peppol network mandate and Canada's digital GST/HST filing rules—to prevent tax base leakage and manage supply chain duties amid evolving trade tariffs.
