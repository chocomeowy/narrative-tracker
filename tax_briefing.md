# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-19 10:01

In late 2026, the international tax architecture continues to consolidate around the OECD's Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax, with over 60 countries and more than 140 jurisdictions enforcing or actively implementing top-up tax rules and GloBE requirements. Simultaneously, jurisdictions such as Singapore and broader ASEAN economies are dynamically adjusting local corporate tax structures and offering targeted grants to preserve FDI competitiveness. The mandatory deployment of e-invoicing and continuous transaction controls (CTCs) continues to accelerate globally, driven by authorities seeking digital compliance enforcement. In parallel, global trade policy remains volatile with ongoing tariff updates and unilateral trade disputes.
