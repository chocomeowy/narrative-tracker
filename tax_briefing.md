# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-25 04:02

As of mid-2026, global tax policy is dominated by the operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% minimum tax framework, now supported by finalized 2026 OECD datasets. Simultaneously, global trade remains high-risk due to persistent tariff friction under executive IEEPA authority and ongoing trilateral trade agreement reviews. Regionally, ASEAN nations are actively calibrating corporate income tax structures—incorporating rebates and substance-based incentives—to balance competitiveness against the global minimum tax backdrop. Digital fiscal administration is accelerating, with revenue authorities, notably Singapore, advancing mandatory e-invoicing and Continuous Transaction Control (CTC) infrastructure to enhance audit efficiency and revenue collection.
