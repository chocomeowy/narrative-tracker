# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-11 16:01

Global corporate tax policy in late 2026 is defined by the operationalization of OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax rules alongside a broad stabilization of headline statutory corporate income tax rates. As statutory rates hold steady, international tax competition has shifted toward targeted R&D incentives and FDI alignment, particularly across regional trade blocs like ASEAN. Concurrently, tax authorities are accelerating mandates for structured, real-time e-invoicing networks (such as Singapore's GST InvoiceNow mandate) and enforcing targeted import tariffs to protect domestic tax bases and streamline indirect revenue collection.
