# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-21 16:01

As of mid-2026, global corporate tax structures and international trade policies continue to undergo fundamental shifts. The OECD's 15% Pillar Two global minimum tax standard remains actively enforced across approximately 140 jurisdictions, significantly impacting multinational effective tax rates and corporate compliance frameworks. Simultaneously, trade volatility remains elevated as judicial challenges to executive tariff authorities under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA) head toward a pivotal Supreme Court decision alongside incoming reciprocal trade policies.
