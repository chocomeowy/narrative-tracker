# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-08 16:01

Late 2026 international corporate tax policy is defined by the dual pressures of OECD Pillar Two operationalization and aggressive tariff recalibrations. Data from the OECD confirms that headline statutory corporate tax rates have plateaued globally, shifting sovereign tax strategy toward local top-up minimum taxes (QDMTTs), targeted enterprise incentives, and continuous transaction controls. Concurrently, tax authorities worldwide are accelerating mandates for structured e-invoicing and real-time indirect tax transmission to safeguard tax bases amidst heightening global trade policy volatility.
