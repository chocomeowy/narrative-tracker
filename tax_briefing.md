# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-10 10:01

Global corporate tax policy in late 2026 is defined by structural tax base realignments, operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax framework, and aggressive trade tariff policy updates. Headline statutory corporate income tax rates have stabilized globally following decades of downward rate competition, shifting government strategy toward targeted incentives and base protection. Simultaneously, digital compliance mandates, real-time e-invoicing integrations, and strict GST/HST filing rules across regions such as North America, India, and ASEAN are forcing multinational enterprises to digitize enterprise resource planning systems and adapt cross-border supply chains.
