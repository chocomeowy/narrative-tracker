# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-10 16:01

Global tax policy in 2026 reflects a dual trajectory of structural rate harmonization and operational digital enforcement. The OECD Pillar Two 15 percent global minimum tax framework is transitioning into statutory domestic enforcement globally through QDMTTs and GloBE rules, forcing territorial tax hubs like Singapore and ASEAN peers to pair baseline statutory corporate rates with structured rebates and non-taxable cash grants. Simultaneously, international trade policy remains volatile with regional duty actions and judicial trade updates, while tax administration is rapidly automating via mandatory Peppol-based Continuous Transaction Controls and e-invoicing mandates.
