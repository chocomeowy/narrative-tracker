# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-23 22:01

In mid-2026, global corporate tax and trade policy is defined by the operationalization of OECD Pillar Two's 15% global minimum tax across roughly 140 jurisdictions, complemented by published 2026 OECD withholding and standard corporate rate datasets. Concurrently, trade policy remains highly volatile, driven by aggressive executive tariff mandates and ongoing court challenges concerning IEEPA authority. Regionally, ASEAN economies are actively adjusting corporate tax structures and rebates to maintain regional investment appeal in the post-Pillar Two environment.
