# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-03 16:01

As of late 2026, the global corporate tax and trade landscape continues to undergo profound structural adaptation. The operationalization of the OECD's 15% global minimum tax (Pillar Two) is firmly established, with multinational enterprises actively realigning systems to comply with GloBE and ETR requirements. Statutory corporate tax rates globally are experiencing stabilization, prompting jurisdictions to refocus on structured substance-based and R&D-focused incentives. Meanwhile, real-time electronic invoicing and digital compliance mandates are rapidly expanding, particularly in Singapore and the broader ASEAN region, transforming indirect tax administration. Simultaneously, escalating trade policy adjustments and tariff volatility continue to drive ongoing customs and supply chain restructuring worldwide.
