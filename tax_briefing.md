# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-26 04:01

The late 2026 corporate tax and international regulatory environment is dominated by the full operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax across over 60 jurisdictions, anchoring statutory corporate rates and shifting jurisdictional tax competition toward substance-based R&D incentives and tax holidays.
