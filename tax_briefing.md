# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-09 16:02

The global tax landscape in late 2026 is characterized by the institutionalization of the OECD Pillar Two framework, with over 60 jurisdictions actively integrating minimum tax rules to ensure a 15% effective floor for large multinationals. Concurrently, tax authorities are aggressively digitizing enforcement, with Singapore's InvoiceNow initiative representing a broader trend of utilizing Peppol-based continuous transaction controls to combat indirect tax leakage. In parallel, international trade remains highly volatile; while domestic statutory corporate tax rates remain relatively stable in major economies, the threat of unilateral trade barriers and emergency-power tariffs continues to drive significant supply chain uncertainty, with key judicial reviews expected to shape future trade policy enforcement.
