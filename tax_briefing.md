# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-02 04:01

The global corporate tax and trade policy landscape in mid-2026 is defined by the operationalization of systemic reforms and legal trade frictions. The G20/OECD Pillar Two 15% minimum effective corporate tax is now fully active across approximately 140 jurisdictions, backed by newly released 2026 OECD Corporate Tax Statistics. This shift has triggered parallel domestic tax restructuring across key regions like ASEAN as countries adapt incentive models and introduce top-up taxes. Concurrently, global trade remains volatile due to escalating reciprocal tariff policies and key legal challenges regarding executive tariff powers in US courts, while tax administrations accelerate continuous transaction controls through mandatory e-invoicing compliance frameworks.
