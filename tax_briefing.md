# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-24 10:01

The global corporate tax landscape in late 2026 is anchored by the full-scale operational implementation of OECD Pillar Two global minimum tax rules, aggressive expansion of real-time digital tax compliance, and escalating international trade tariff volatility. With over 60 jurisdictions actively enforcing the 15% GloBE floor, cross-border tax competition has shifted away from statutory rate reductions toward non-rate structural incentives, substance-based credits, and regional tax harmonization—most notably across the ASEAN region. Concurrently, revenue authorities are expanding mandatory real-time e-invoicing and digital GST/VAT reporting to minimize compliance gaps, while protective tariffs and supply chain realignment force multinational enterprises to re-evaluate landed costs and customs compliance strategies.
