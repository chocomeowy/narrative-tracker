# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-22 16:01

The global tax landscape in 2026 is characterized by the entrenched implementation of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax, with over 140 jurisdictions actively applying it. This has led to a strategic pivot in investment hubs like Singapore and other ASEAN-6 nations, who are now leveraging structured corporate income tax rebates and non-taxable cash grants to maintain FDI attractiveness, as traditional tax incentives are curtailed. Concurrently, tax administrations worldwide are rapidly adopting digital compliance measures, prominently featuring continuous transaction controls (CTCs) and mandatory Peppol-standardized e-invoicing networks, exemplified by Singapore's InvoiceNow initiative. The international trade arena remains volatile, marked by ongoing unilateral tariff escalations and high-stakes executive trade negotiations, underscoring a persistently high-friction environment.
