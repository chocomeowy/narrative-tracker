# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-02 10:01

Global corporate tax policy in late 2026 continues to be anchored by the institutionalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax framework and the stabilization of statutory corporate income tax rates. As nominal rates arrest their historical downward trend, revenue authorities worldwide are shifting their primary policy instruments toward substance-based incentive realignments, enhanced R&D credits, and rigorous base erosion monitoring. Concurrently, major economic hubs and ASEAN jurisdictions are scaling mandatory real-time electronic invoicing networks to eliminate tax leakage and automate transaction-level compliance.
