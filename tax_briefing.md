# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-20 04:01

The global tax landscape in late 2026 is experiencing a phase of active, operational transformation. The OECD's Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax is transitioning fully from policy to localized execution, supported by over 60 jurisdictions implementing GloBE rules and the release of the OECD's flagship 2026 Corporate Tax Statistics. ASEAN hubs, particularly Singapore, are realigning their incentives to shield foreign direct investment without violating minimum tax rules. Simultaneously, the proliferation of mandatory e-invoicing networks like Singapore's InvoiceNow illustrates a global structural push toward continuous transaction controls. This compliance complexity is further complicated by escalating trade volatility, driven by high-profile unilateral tariffs.
