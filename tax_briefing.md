# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-21 16:01

The global corporate tax landscape in late 2026 is characterized by the full operationalization of OECD Pillar Two, now enacted in over 60 jurisdictions, which is harmonizing minimum effective tax rates for multinational enterprises. This is complemented by a surge in real-time digital tax enforcement, exemplified by Singapore's e-invoicing mandates, which are enhancing indirect tax compliance and visibility. Concurrently, trade policy remains volatile, with unilateral tariff adjustments and evolving duty structures creating ongoing complexities for international supply chains and cross-border trade compliance. The corporate tax rate environment globally shows a stabilization after a period of decline, with statutory rates arresting their downward trend.
