# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-04 04:01

The 2026 corporate and international tax policy landscape is defined by the operationalization of OECD Pillar Two global minimum tax standards, driving extensive domestic tax code overhauls across approximately 140 jurisdictions. Sovereign governments are actively calibrating statutory corporate income tax rates and incentive regimes, particularly within regional blocs like ASEAN, to maintain economic competitiveness while preventing base erosion.
