# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-18 16:01

As of late 2026, the global corporate tax landscape has reached a new steady state, dominated by the institutionalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% effective minimum tax. This has effectively ended the multi-decade 'race to the bottom' regarding statutory corporate tax rates. Concurrently, nations—particularly within the ASEAN bloc—are moving beyond traditional tax holidays toward sophisticated, Pillar Two-compliant investment incentives like refundable tax credits. In the realm of tax administration, the shift toward mandatory real-time e-invoicing has become an irreversible standard for compliance. Simultaneously, global trade remains defined by active and volatile protectionist tariff cycles, as governments increasingly leverage customs policy as a primary tool for industrial and geopolitical strategy.
