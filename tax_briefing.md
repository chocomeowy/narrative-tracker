# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-28 10:01

Mid-2026 tax policy is characterized by structural realignments across multinational corporate taxation, escalating global trade barriers, and digital-first indirect tax enforcement. Pillar Two's 15% global minimum tax is driving countries—particularly within the competitive ASEAN zone—to restructure corporate income tax incentives while protecting local revenues. Concurrently, major unilateral tariff regimes undergo intense legal scrutiny, highlighted by US Court of International Trade rulings on executive authorities like IEEPA and Section 232. Operationally, digital tax administration is solidifying through mandatory, Peppol-compliant e-invoicing initiatives like Singapore's newly structured multi-year InvoiceNow rollout designed to structurally curb tax leakage.
