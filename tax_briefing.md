# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-18 16:01

In mid-2026, global corporate tax policy and international trade frameworks are undergoing structural transformations and increased judicial scrutiny. The OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax framework is operational across approximately 140 Inclusive Framework jurisdictions, driving baseline corporate tax adjustments and domestic top-up tax mechanisms. Meanwhile, international trade faces operational uncertainty as legal challenges against executive emergency tariff powers (IEEPA) move through U.S. federal courts. In the Asia-Pacific region, ASEAN financial hubs like Singapore are maintaining headline statutory corporate tax rates (17%) supplemented by targeted cash rebates and incentives. Concurrently, tax administrations are formalizing digital compliance through continuous transaction controls and mandatory Peppol-based e-invoicing, exemplified by Singapore's phased GST InvoiceNow rollout through 2031.
