# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-11 16:01

The corporate and international tax landscape of 2026 is defined by the operationalization of structural tax changes and escalating trade frictions. The OECD Pillar Two framework is actively reshaping multinational profit allocation with over 140 nations adopting the 15% global minimum tax, heavily impacting US companies operating abroad. Structurally, statutory rate adjustments continue globally, marked by Portugal's phased corporate income tax reduction and the US legislative extensions via the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.
