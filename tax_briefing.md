# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-07-30 04:01

Global tax policy in mid-2026 continues to be defined by the operational enforcement of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax alongside domestic corporate income tax adjustments across key jurisdictions. Regional financial hubs, particularly in ASEAN, are strategically adapting through corporate income tax rebates and structural incentive reforms to remain competitive while staying Pillar Two compliant. Concurrently, international trade is buffeted by evolving Section 232 tariffs and significant legal developments concerning tariff reliquidation rulings. To combat tax leakage, tax authorities are aggressively pursuing digital transformation, enforcing mandatory Peppol-based e-invoicing and real-time transactional reporting compliance.
