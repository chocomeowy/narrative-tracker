# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-08 04:01

While Pillar Two rules are actively enforced across Europe, APAC, and ASEAN+3 jurisdictions to neutralize profit shifting, national authorities are recalibrating statutory corporate rates, cash grants, and target rebates—such as Singapore's 50% CIT rebate for YA2026 and Portugal's gradual CIT reductions—to preserve regional competitiveness.
