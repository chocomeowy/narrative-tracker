# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-21 04:01

As of late 2026, global corporate tax policy is defined by dual pressures: domestic adoption of real-time digital tax enforcement and rising international trade policy volatility. The operationalization of OECD Pillar Two global minimum tax rules continues to reshape multinational planning, while tax administrations, particularly across ASEAN, are aggressively mandating Peppol-based real-time e-invoicing networks (such as Singapore's InvoiceNow GST mandate). Simultaneously, a resurgence in unilateral import tariff policies and trade protectionism is introducing substantial compliance and supply chain complexities for cross-border operations.
