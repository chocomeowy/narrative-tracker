# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-17 10:01

As of late 2026, global tax policy is characterized by the institutionalization of the OECD Pillar Two framework and a stabilization of statutory corporate tax rates. Nations are increasingly shifting focus from aggressive rate competition to the operationalization of digital tax administration and the use of fiscal policy for industrial protectionism. Trade policy is dominated by rising tariff volatility, while ASEAN jurisdictions are actively re-engineering incentive regimes to remain competitive within the post-Pillar Two landscape.
