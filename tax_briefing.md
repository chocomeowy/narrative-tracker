# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-31 22:01

In late 2026, global corporate tax policy continues to center on the operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax, driving domestic top-up taxes and rigorous ETR monitoring. Meanwhile, statutory corporate income tax rates have largely plateaued across major jurisdictions and regional blocs like ASEAN, prompting tax authorities to pivot toward substance-based incentives, R&D credits, and targeted IP regimes. Concurrently, aggressive digital tax enforcement is expanding rapidly worldwide, highlighted by phased mandatory real-time electronic invoicing frameworks such as Singapore's GST InvoiceNow rollout.
