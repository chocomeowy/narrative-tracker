# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-07-31 04:01

Global corporate tax policy in mid-2026 is defined by the active operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax framework, establishing a mandatory global tax floor for multinational enterprises. In response, regional hubs across Singapore and ASEAN are adjusting domestic tax structures using targeted CIT rebates and structured incentives to maintain investment attractiveness without violating Pillar Two rules. Simultaneously, international trade faces significant friction from expanded U.S. Section 232 tariffs on metals, pharmaceutical duty measures, and lingering unilateral tariffs. To optimize revenue collection and audit oversight, tax administrations worldwide are accelerating mandatory digital tax compliance via standardized Peppol e-invoicing networks like Singapore's InvoiceNow initiative.
