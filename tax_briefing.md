# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-22 10:01

The global tax landscape in 2026 is defined by the widespread operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax across over 140 jurisdictions, reinforced by the release of the 2026 OECD Corporate Tax Statistics. As traditional tax incentives face pressure under the global minimum tax framework, regional investment hubs like Singapore and other ASEAN-6 economies are shifting toward structured corporate income tax rebates and non-taxable cash grants to preserve FDI attractiveness. Concurrently, tax administrations are aggressively scaling digital compliance through continuous transaction controls (CTCs) and mandated Peppol-standardized e-invoicing networks such as Singapore's InvoiceNow. International trade policy remains high-friction due to persistent unilateral tariff escalations and executive trade negotiations.
