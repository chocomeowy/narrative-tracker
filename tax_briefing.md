# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-09-27 10:01

In 2026, corporate and international tax policy is shaped by the operationalization of the 15% global minimum tax under OECD Pillar Two across approximately 140 jurisdictions, forcing regional tax restructuring to balance domestic top-up taxes with FDI competitiveness. Simultaneously, tax administrations are accelerating digital compliance through mandatory Peppol-based e-invoicing and continuous transaction controls, led by Singapore's GST InvoiceNow rollout targeting complete taxpayer enforcement by 2031. On the trade front, volatile unilateral tariff escalations (including Section 232 adjustments and contested IEEPA tariffs) continue to face high-profile judicial challenges in trade courts, keeping cross-border supply chain taxation costs elevated.
