# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-07-31 10:01

Global corporate tax policy in mid-2026 continues to be anchored by the ongoing operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax framework, which mandates an effective tax floor for large multinational enterprises. Concurrently, regional hubs and ASEAN jurisdictions are adapting their local tax architectures, balancing structural corporate income tax rates, targeted incentives, and CIT rebates to remain competitive without violating GloBE rules. In the trade sector, international friction remains elevated due to ongoing U.S. Section 232 tariff updates and duties on critical inputs and pharmaceuticals. Furthermore, tax administrations worldwide are prioritizing digital enforcement, expanding real-time tax compliance models through standardized Peppol e-invoicing networks.
