# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-29 16:02

As of late 2026, the global corporate tax landscape is characterized by the maturation of the OECD Pillar Two framework, with over 60 jurisdictions having operationalized the 15% global minimum tax, effectively capping the race to the bottom in statutory rates. Simultaneously, tax administrations are moving beyond voluntary adoption of digital processes to mandatory, real-time invoice clearance systems, most notably through the expansion of Peppol-based networks like Singapore's InvoiceNow. While statutory tax rates remain generally stable, the focus of multinational tax strategy has shifted decisively toward substance-based incentive alignment and managing the complexities of evolving international trade tariffs driven by regional security and protectionist policies.
