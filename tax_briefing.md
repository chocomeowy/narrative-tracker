# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-11-15 22:01

As of late 2026, global tax policy is characterized by the widespread operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax framework, effectively halting the multi-decade race-to-the-bottom in statutory corporate rates. Taxing authorities are shifting focus from headline rate reductions toward structural base erosion protection, targeted non-rate incentives (such as ASEAN reinvestment allowances and qualified tax credits), and real-time electronic invoicing mandates for continuous VAT/GST enforcement. Concurrently, rising protectionist tariff cycles are reintroducing trade frictions and elevating customs compliance to a core element of cross-border tax strategy.
