# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-04 04:01

Global corporate tax frameworks are undergoing structural shifts in 2026. The implementation of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax is transitioning from theory to statutory reality, with countries actively passing GloBE-aligned laws and the OECD releasing its latest Corporate Tax Statistics. Concurrently, jurisdictions, particularly in the ASEAN region, are adjusting their statutory corporate tax rates and launching special incentives to offset Pillar Two rules. In tandem, digital tax compliance is accelerating as tax authorities globally mandate system-to-system, real-time transaction reporting, notably through Singapore's phased Peppol-based InvoiceNow framework.
