# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-08-07 10:01

Global tax policy in mid-2026 continues to be dominated by the operationalization of the OECD Pillar Two 15 percent global minimum tax across participating jurisdictions. Simultaneously, international trade disputes and legal challenges surrounding sweeping tariff measures—such as court decisions regarding Section 122 and IEEPA—create ongoing volatility for multinational supply chains. Furthermore, regional tax reforms and aggressive expansions of continuous transaction controls and e-invoicing mandates, notably in Singapore, are reshaping digital tax compliance and corporate reporting requirements worldwide.
