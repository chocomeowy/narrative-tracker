# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-07-27 16:01

Mid-2026 international tax dynamics continue to be shaped by the active statutory enforcement of the OECD Pillar Two 15% minimum tax and aggressive unilateral trade tariff updates, particularly expanded U.S. Section 232 measures affecting metals and pharmaceuticals alongside emerging legal challenges to executive tariff actions. Regional financial hubs across ASEAN, notably Singapore, are recalibrating corporate tax incentives, rebates, and tax frameworks to preserve FDI competitiveness while implementing top-up rules.
