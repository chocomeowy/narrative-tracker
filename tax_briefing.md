# Tax Intelligence Briefing - 2026-10-04 22:01

The corporate and international tax policy landscape in late 2026 is defined by the widespread statutory execution of the OECD Pillar Two 15% global minimum tax, backed by comprehensive tracking in the newly released 2026 Corporate Tax Statistics. This is accompanied by a globally coordinated tightening of statutory and withholding tax rates, including adjustments to federal, state, and pass-through tax structures. In parallel, tax authorities are aggressively digitising enforcement through mandatory, real-time transaction monitoring, transitionally shifting structured e-invoicing frameworks (such as Peppol and Singapore's InvoiceNow) from administrative options to core compliance mandates. Regionally, the ASEAN bloc continues its strategic tax policy recalibration to balance international anti-BEPS compliance with regional investment competitiveness.
